* **Proxy**: interception and modification of requests and responses while interacting with web applications.
- **Repeater**: capturing, modifying, and resending the same request multiple times. 
- **Intruder**: allows for spraying endpoints with requests. It is commonly utilized for brute-force attacks or fuzzing endpoints.
- **Decoder**: decode captured information or encode payloads before sending them to the target. 
- **Comparer**: comparison of two pieces of data at either the word or byte level. 
- **Sequencer**:assessing the randomness of tokens, such as session cookie values or other supposedly randomly generated data.
