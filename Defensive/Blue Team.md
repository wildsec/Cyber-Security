# Blue Team

Defensive security team responsible for protecting an organization's assets and infrastructure.

## SOC (Security Operations Center)
[SOC](SOC/General.md) - The central hub for security operations.

* **L1 Analysts**: Junior members who triage alerts and pass complex cases to L2
* **L2 Analysts**: Experienced members who investigate more advanced attacks
* **Engineers**: Experts in configuring security tools like [EDR](EDR.md) or [General](SIEM/General.md)
* **Manager**: A person who manages the whole SOC team

## CIRT (Cyber Incident Response Team)
* CIRT Manager
* Forensic Lead
* Threat Intel Expert
* Threat Hunting Expert
* Malware Analyst

## Specialized Defensive Roles
- **Digital Forensics Analyst**: Uncover hidden threats in disk and memory. See [Digital Forensics](Forensic/Digital%20Forensics.md)
- **Threat Intelligence Analyst**: Gather data about emerging threat groups
- **AppSec Engineer**: Maintain a secure software development lifecycle. See [SDLC](../Principles/SDLC.md)
- **AI Researcher**: Study AI threats and how to defend against them
