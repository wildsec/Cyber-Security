# Pyramid of Pain

The Pyramid of Pain is a model that ranks different types of threat intelligence indicators by their value to defenders and the cost to attackers.

![[Pasted image 20260303191347.png]]

## Levels (Bottom to Top)

1. **Hash Values** - Easy for attackers to change
2. **IP Addresses** - Can be changed quickly
3. **Domain Names** - Requires some effort to change
4. **Network/Host Artifacts** - Harder for attackers to modify
5. **Tools** - Significant effort to change
6. **TTPs (Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures)** - Most valuable and hardest for attackers to change
