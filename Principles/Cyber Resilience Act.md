CRA - EU Regulation 2024/2847

### Overview  
The **Cyber Resilience Act** is a European Union regulation (effective 2024) that mandates cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements. It creates a legal framework for cybersecurity throughout a product's lifecycle.  
  
### Scope & Classification  
- **Class I**: Important products (browsers, operating systems, firewalls, VPNs)  
- **Class II**: Critical products (hardware security modules, industrial control systems, smartcards)  
- **Exemptions**: Military, aviation, medical devices (covered elsewhere), open-source (with caveats)  
  
### Key Requirements  
1. **Secure by Design**: Cybersecurity built in from development  
2. **Vulnerability Management**: 24-hour reporting of actively exploited vulnerabilities  
3. **Patch Availability**: Security updates for 5 years or product lifetime  
4. **Conformity Assessment**: Third-party assessment for critical products  
5. **CE Marking**: Cybersecurity becomes part of CE conformity  
  
### Cybersecurity Application  
- Shifts security "left" into product development  
- Creates liability for manufacturers of insecure products  
- Forces supply chain transparency  
- Establishes EU-wide baseline for product security
