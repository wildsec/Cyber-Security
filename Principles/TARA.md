Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment
### Overview  
**TARA** (Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment) is a methodology defined in **ISO/SAE 21434** for automotive cybersecurity. It provides a systematic approach to identifying, evaluating, and treating cybersecurity risks in vehicles and components.  
  
### Process Flow  
1. **Asset Identification**: What needs protection (ECUs, data, functions)  
2. **Threat Scenario Identification**: How assets could be attacked  
3. **Impact Assessment**: Damage potential (Safety, Financial, Operational, Privacy)  
4. **Attack Path Analysis**: How threat scenarios could be realized  
5. **Attack Feasibility Rating**: Likelihood of successful attack (based on elapsed time, expertise, equipment, window of opportunity)  
6. **Risk Determination**: Impact × Feasibility  
7. **Risk Treatment**: Accept, Reduce, Transfer, or Avoid  
  
### Core Principles  
1. **Systematic Analysis**: Structured, repeatable process  
2. **Asset-Centric**: Focus on what matters most  
3. **Feasibility-Based**: Realistic assessment of attack likelihood  
4. **Risk-Driven**: Decisions based on quantified risk  
  
### Cybersecurity Application  
- Mandatory for automotive type approval (UN R155)  
- Applicable beyond automotive (any cyber-physical system)  
- Integrates with ISO 26262 (functional safety)  
- Drives security requirements specification
