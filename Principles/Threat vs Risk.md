 
| **Threat** | **Risk** |  
|------------|----------|  
| **What CAN go wrong** | **What WILL go wrong** (and impact) |  
| **Potential danger** | **Likelihood × Impact** |  
| **Actor or event** | **Probability of loss** |  
  
---  
  
### Definitions  
  
| Term | Definition | Example |  
|------|------------|---------|  
| **Threat** | Any circumstance or event with potential to harm assets | Ransomware group, insider, natural disaster, software bug |  
| **Risk** | The *probability* that a threat exploits a vulnerability causing damage | "30% chance ransomware encrypts production DB this year → €2M loss" |  
  
---  
  
### The Relationship  
  

Risk = Threat × Vulnerability × Impact
       ─────                ─────────
       (Actor/Event)        (Consequence)
              ↓
         Exploits weakness
              ↓
         Results in loss

  
---  
  
### Simple Analogy  
  
| Analogy | Threat | Risk |  
|---------|--------|------|  
| **House** | Burglar (actor), Storm (event) | "5% chance of break-in → $10K stolen" |  
| **Road** | Drunk driver | "Probability of accident × injury cost" |  
| **Body** | Virus/bacteria | "Chance of infection × severity" |  
  
---  
  
### Key Differences  
  
| Aspect | Threat | Risk |  
|--------|--------|------|  
| **Nature** | External or inherent | Calculated/measured |  
| **Control** | You can't eliminate all threats | You CAN reduce/transfer/accept risk |  
| **Focus** | Identification | Quantification & management |  
| **Question** | *"What could hurt us?"* | *"How bad and how likely?"* |  
  
---  
  
### In Practice ([[TARA]] Example)  
  
| Component | Description |  
|-----------|-------------|  
| **Threat** | "Remote attacker exploits unpatched CVE in ECU" |  
| **Vulnerability** | Missing security patch |  
| **Impact** | Vehicle control loss, safety critical |  
| **Risk** | **High**: Likely × Catastrophic → Requires immediate mitigation |  
  
---  
  
## Bottom Line  
  
> **Threat = The "what"** (danger exists)  
> **Risk = The "so what"** (measure & decide)  
  
You **identify threats** to know your enemies.  
You **assess risk** to know where to spend your security budget.
